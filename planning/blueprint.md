## 5) DESIGN SYSTEM
- **Palette:** `night #06060B` bg · `ink #0D0D17` panels · `moon #F2E8D5` primary text ·
  `thread #C4453A` (rakhi red — stamps/accents) · `gold #D9A441` (thread/highlights) ·
  `mist #8E8CA3` secondary. Dark, cinematic, zero pastels.
- **Type:** **Fraunces** (display serif — big emotional lines, italics for soft moments) ·
  **Space Grotesk** (UI) · **Space Mono** (exhibit tags / stamps / timestamps) ·
  **Kalam** (handwritten letter — unless you photograph real handwriting, which is 10× better).
- **Motion:** scroll = timeline (GSAP ScrollTrigger, pinned + scrubbed). Roast content
  **snaps** fast (stamps, 0.3s). Emotional content **floats** slow (word-by-word, 1.5–3s).
  Everything breathes: moon drift, stars twinkle on 4–7s loops. `prefers-reduced-motion` = static fallback.
- **Imagery:** all photos graded into ONE look — moody, deep blacks, warm moonlight
  highlights, film grain overlay (CSS, not baked), polaroid + tape, `2:22 AM` timestamps.
- **Sound:** night ambience (crickets + distant traffic, very low) from the gate onwards ·
  soft UI ticks on taps · **music enters at Act III turn only** — silence before it is deliberate.
  Mute button always visible. Everything self-hosted, ~250KB total.
